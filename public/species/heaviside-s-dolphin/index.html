Heaviside's Dolphin: where it lives

Cephalorhynchus heavisidii · Xevisayda delfini

Heaviside's Dolphin (Cephalorhynchus heavisidii) has been recorded 473 times in the Ocean Biodiversity Information System, from 1969 to 2026, in 16 places at sea, usually 0 to 10 m deep.
