What lives in the Red Sea (Hurghada, Egypt)?

Qizil dengiz · 27.5° N, 34.2° E

1,268 species of sea life have been recorded in the 1.4° square around this point (about 150 km across), from 36,258 records made from 1823 to 2026. By the Chao1 estimate that is about 74 % of the species more looking would probably find.
