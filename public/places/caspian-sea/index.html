What lives in the Caspian Sea (south-east coast)?

Kaspiy dengizi · 37.5° N, 53.5° E

110 species of sea life have been recorded in the 1.4° square around this point (about 150 km across), from 349 records made from 1961 to 2026. By the Chao1 estimate that is about 51 % of the species more looking would probably find.
