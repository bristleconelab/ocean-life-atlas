What lives in the Antarctic Peninsula?

Antarktida yarim oroli · 64.8° S, 63.5° W

1,217 species of sea life have been recorded in the 1.4° square around this point (about 150 km across), from 34,120 records made from 1872 to 2026. By the Chao1 estimate that is about 79 % of the species more looking would probably find.
