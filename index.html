    About this atlas

    Ocean Life Atlas shows what people have recorded living in the sea. Click a spot to see the species found within a square about 150 km across, or search for an animal or a sea to see where it has been seen, how deep it lives and how it migrates.

    A list here is a record of what was found, not a census. Most of the ocean has hardly been sampled, so a short list usually means few people looked, not that little lives there. Switch on Where scientists have looked to see the gaps. Records OBIS flags as lying on land (a museum specimen filed under a city, a river record) are left out everywhere, and so are species that live only on land or in fresh water.

    Where the data comes from

    • Species records, ranges and depths: OBIS, the Ocean Biodiversity Information System (each dataset keeps its own licence, mostly CC BY or CC0).
    • Names and search: World Register of Marine Species (CC BY 4.0).
    • Photos: Wikimedia Commons, credited on each photo; common names and links: Wikidata (CC0), queried through QLever.
    • Summaries: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Sea names: Marine Regions (CC BY 4.0).
    • Globe imagery: NASA Blue Marble through GIBS (public domain). Borders and place names: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), tiles by OpenFreeMap. Place search: Marine Regions gazetteer.
    • Red List status: IUCN categories as provided by OBIS.
    • Migration networks: MiCO, Bentley et al. (2025) Nature Communications, data Zenodo (CC0). Tagged-animal tracks: OBIS telemetry records (satellite and acoustic tags), credited per study on each species page.

    Privacy

    This site has no server of its own, no cookies and no analytics. Your browser loads the data straight from the services above (OBIS, WoRMS, Wikidata and QLever, Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia, Marine Regions, NASA GIBS and OpenFreeMap), so each of them sees your IP address and what you look up, as with any website. Near me uses your location only in your browser: what is sent to OBIS is the outline of the square, as for any click.
