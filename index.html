    About this atlas

    Ocean Life Atlas shows what people have recorded living in the sea. Click a spot to see the species found within a square about 150 km across, or search for an animal or a sea to see where it has been seen, how deep it lives and how it migrates.

    A list here is a record of what was found, not a census. Most of the ocean has hardly been sampled, so a short list usually means few people looked, not that little lives there. Switch on Where scientists have looked to see the gaps, and how long ago each square was last looked at. Records OBIS flags as lying on land (a museum specimen filed under a city, a river record) are left out everywhere, and so are species that live only on land or in fresh water.

    Where the data comes from

    • Species records, ranges and depths: OBIS, the Ocean Biodiversity Information System (each dataset keeps its own licence, mostly CC BY or CC0).
    • Names and search: World Register of Marine Species (CC BY 4.0).
    • Photos, sounds, body size and lifespan: Wikimedia Commons, credited on each file; common names and links: Wikidata (CC0), queried through QLever.
    • Summaries: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Sea names and place search: Marine Regions (CC BY 4.0).
    • Globe imagery: NASA Blue Marble through GIBS (public domain); close up, Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 by EOX (CC BY 4.0, contains modified Copernicus Sentinel data 2016). Borders and place names: OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), tiles by OpenFreeMap.
    • Red List status: IUCN categories as provided by OBIS.
    • Migration networks: MiCO, Bentley et al. (2025) Nature Communications, data Zenodo (CC0). Tagged-animal tracks: OBIS telemetry records (satellite and acoustic tags), credited per study on each species page.

    Privacy

    This site has no server of its own, no cookies and no analytics; it remembers only your language choice, in your browser. Your browser loads the data straight from the services above (OBIS, WoRMS, Wikidata and QLever, Wikimedia Commons, Wikipedia, Marine Regions, NASA GIBS, EOX and OpenFreeMap), so each of them sees your IP address and what you look up, as with any website. Near me uses your location only in your browser: what is sent to OBIS is the outline of the square, as for any click.

    Code: github.com/bristleconelab/ocean-life-atlas (MIT). Sister projects: Seafloor Atlas, Fauna Atlas (animals on land, in rivers and at sea).

    Atlas haqida

    Ocean Life Atlas odamlar dengizda qayd etgan hayotni koʻrsatadi. Taxminan 150 km lik kvadratda topilgan turlarni koʻrish uchun biror joyni bosing yoki hayvon yoki dengizni qidirib, u qayerda koʻrilgani, qanchalik chuqurda yashashi va qanday migratsiya qilishini biling.

    Bu yerdagi roʻyxat sanoq emas, topilgan narsalar yozuvi. Okeanning katta qismi deyarli oʻrganilmagan, shuning uchun qisqa roʻyxat odatda kam hayotni emas, kam odam qaraganini bildiradi. Boʻshliqlarni va har bir kvadratga oxirgi marta qachon qaralganini koʻrish uchun Olimlar qayerni oʻrgangan qatlamini yoqing. OBIS quruqlikda deb belgilagan yozuvlar (shahar nomi bilan saqlangan muzey namunasi, daryo yozuvi) va faqat quruqlikda yoki chuchuk suvda yashaydigan turlar hamma joyda chiqarib tashlangan.

    Maʼlumotlar manbasi

    • Tur yozuvlari, tarqalish va chuqurlik: OBIS (har bir toʻplam oʻz litsenziyasida, asosan CC BY yoki CC0).
    • Nomlar va qidiruv: WoRMS (CC BY 4.0).
    • Suratlar, ovozlar, oʻlcham va umr: Wikimedia Commons, har bir faylda muallifi bilan; nomlar va havolalar: Wikidata (CC0), QLever orqali.
    • Qisqa maʼlumot: Wikipedia (CC BY-SA 4.0). Dengiz nomlari va joy qidiruvi: Marine Regions (CC BY 4.0).
    • Globus tasviri: NASA Blue Marble, GIBS orqali; yaqindan — EOX ning Sentinel-2 cloudless 2016 (CC BY 4.0). Chegaralar va joy nomlari: OpenStreetMap (ODbL), OpenFreeMap orqali.
    • Qizil kitob holati: OBIS bergan IUCN toifalari.
    • Migratsiya tarmoqlari: MiCO, Bentley va boshq. (2025), maʼlumot Zenodo (CC0). Belgilangan hayvonlar yoʻllari: OBIS telemetriya yozuvlari, har bir tur sahifasida tadqiqot nomi bilan.

    Maxfiylik

    Saytning oʻz serveri, cookie va analitikasi yoʻq; brauzeringizda faqat tanlagan tilingiz saqlanadi. Maʼlumotlar yuqoridagi xizmatlardan toʻgʻridan-toʻgʻri brauzeringizga yuklanadi, shuning uchun ularning har biri har qanday saytdagi kabi IP manzilingiz va nimani qidirayotganingizni koʻradi.
